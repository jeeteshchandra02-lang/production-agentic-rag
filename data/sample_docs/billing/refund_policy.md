# Subscription refund policy

Enterprise annual subscriptions can be cancelled within 30 calendar days from
the contract start date for a full refund if onboarding has not been completed.

After the first 30 days, annual subscription fees are non-refundable.
Customers can choose not to renew at the end of the contracted period.

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. The cancellation becomes effective
at the end of the current billing cycle and unused days are not refunded.

Customers should contact the account team for billing disputes or duplicate
charges.
